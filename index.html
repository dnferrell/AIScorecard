      • Something repetitive
      • Something you are procrastinating on
      • Something you don’t know how to start

      You can ask AI: “I am a [job title] and I am looking for ways that generative AI can help me do my job. Ask me three questions about my day-to-day and help me identify some options.”

      The guiding principle

      Use AI when risk is low, it saves time, and you can fully review and own the result. Avoid AI when stakes are high, accuracy is critical, or you would not stand behind the output.
